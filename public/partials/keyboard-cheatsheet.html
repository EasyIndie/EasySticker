Keyboard

键盘操作速查表 / Keyboard Cheatsheet

  • 风格芯片：左右/上下箭头移动焦点；Home/End 快速跳首/跳尾；Enter/空格切换选中；至少保留 1 种风格。
  • 生成快捷键：Ctrl + Enter 在生成表单内快速触发生成（遵循必填校验）。
  • 跳到主要内容：Tab 聚焦左上角“跳到主要内容”后按 Enter 跳转到主区域。
  • Style chips: Arrow Left/Right/Up/Down move focus; Home/End jump to first/last; Enter/Space toggle selection; keep at least one style selected.
  • Generate shortcut: Ctrl + Enter triggers generation within the form (respects required field validation).
  • Skip to main: Tab to the “Skip to main content” link at top-left and press Enter to jump to the main area.
